# Incubator

The incubator is a place for ideas that feel valuable but are not yet ready to become workshop standards, processes or commitments.

Ideas belong here without needing to be fully designed or justified. They can remain, evolve, combine with other ideas, or disappear as practical experience teaches us more.

## Commissioned Instrument Story

Explore creating a handover document for each commissioned instrument that records the story of how the instrument came to be.

It could capture:

- the owner's original goals and ideas;
- important design decisions;
- alternatives that were considered;
- materials and construction choices;
- photographs from the design and build process;
- discoveries, experiments and significant moments during the build;
- the reasoning behind the features that make the instrument unique.

The document should be more than a technical specification or build record. It should preserve the provenance and story of the instrument and show how the owner participated in its creation.

A future owner should be able to show someone both an exceptional instrument and the story behind it.

That story may itself become part of what makes someone else want to begin their own instrument design journey.
